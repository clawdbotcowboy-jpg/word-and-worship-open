# Published rules

This folder is a public adaptation of Word & Worship’s Scripture & Doctrine Gate rules (v1, dated 19 November 2026, with later confirmed answers through 20 November 2026, America/Phoenix).

It is a doctrine and product-rules document, not a dump of internal operations. Staff names, internal systems, unpublished work, and private infrastructure are omitted. The main application remains private.

These rules sit under locks that do not change:

- Scripture in the product is the World English Bible only
- No ESV text
- No Matthew Henry
- No voice for God or Jesus
- AI Scripture narration is Plus-only
- Crisis help comes first: the 988 handoff is never blocked or delayed
